## Steps to make a Peanut Butter Sandwich

## Assumption
- person making the sandwich knows what a sandwich is
- sandwich maker knows how to use a knife
- sandwich maker can procure peanut butter and bread and has peanut butter, bread, and utensils on hand

Ingredients and tools needed:
- two slices of bread
- a knife
- a jar of you favorite peanut butter
- a plate

## Steps
Steps to assemble the sandwich:
- remove the two slices of bread from the loaf of bread 
- lay the two slices of bread side by side on the plate on table or counter top
- grab peanut butter jar from cabinet or counter top
- open the peanut butter jar
- pick up the knife
- use the knife to grab about a tablespoon and a half of peanut butter from the jar
- spread the peanut butter from the knife on to one slice of bread covering edge to edge
- place the knife back on the table 
- place and align the bare slice of bread on top of the slice covered in peanut butter making sure the peanut better is in between the two slices
- put the lid back on the peanut better jar and back in the cabinet
- put knife in the sink

## Done when 

Peanut butter sandwich is complete when peanut butter is spread evenly between the two slices on bread that are aligned on top of each other
