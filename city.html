Wilmington, Delaware, USA

History

Wilmington’s status as Delaware’s largest city and economic engine gives us a big city feel while our scale and walkability preserve some small-town charm.

Wilmington, Delaware is famous as the "Corporate Capital of the World" because over 60% of Fortune 500 companies choose to incorporate there

Continuous Broadway: The Playhouse on Rodney Square opened in 1913 and remains the oldest theater on a continuous Broadway touring circuit.

Wilmington was designated as the Delaware American World War II Heritage City on December 28, 2023.
